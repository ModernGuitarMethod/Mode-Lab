How to use

Natural notes on the neck

Fret Lab trains notes on the neck and staff with short games, plus scale studies (sequences, pyramid, broken 3rds).

  1. Read the big gold box: what this game is and what you do.
  2. Practice may teach with extra help. Test me = no hints.
  3. Start here: Staff → neck. Read the note and tap the exact fret (the octave matters).
  4. Scale studies (works on every family/mode here): Sequence in 3s = 123 234 345 … then back down; Pyramid = 121, 12321, … up to the octave, every group ends on 1; Broken 3rds = 13 24 35 … then back down (called Skip-a-step in 5-, 6- and 8-note scales, where 1-3 is not always a 3rd).
  5. Multi-location: same pitch in many places — tap every match. The neck is blank until you tap.
  6. Guitar music is written an octave higher than it sounds. Open strings sit left of the nut.
  7. Key, family and mode carry over from Mode Lab, and back again when you open Mode Lab or Jam.
