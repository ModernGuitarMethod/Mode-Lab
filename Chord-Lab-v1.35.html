How to use

Chord Lab is your grip gym. Hear the chord, see the shape, then take it to Jam or Songwriter.

  1. Pick Key.
  2. Pick Family and Mode (dropdowns keep the neck big).
  3. Choose a chord from the scale (qualities follow the mode — Dorian i is m7).
  4. Extras in dropdowns: instrument, strings, voicing, CAGED form.
  5. Tap Play once to unlock SoundFont audio.
  6. Compare alternate grips in the strip.
  7. Open Jam or Songwriter — key travels with you.
