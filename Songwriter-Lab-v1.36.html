How to use

Songwriter Lab turns a key into a lyric + chord lead sheet.

  1. Fill Song info and Copyright — then it auto-collapses to one bar so brainstorm and lead sheet get the height.
  2. Pick Key / Mode (same handoff as Mode Lab).
  3. Build the form with section chips; drag to reorder.
  4. Brainstorm 5W+H and section lyrics.
  5. Drop chords on the lead sheet.
  6. Print: title centered, Music and lyrics by top right, copyright at the bottom — no CUA codes.
