How to use it. Jam Lab sits next to Mode Lab. Blues is only I7–IV7–V7 (12-bar when the loop is long enough). Jazz defaults to ii–V–I. Funk vamps on one or two chords. Metal sits on i–♭VI–♭VII power chords. Country is I–IV–V boom-chick. Folk is I–V–vi–IV with quieter drums. Rock is I–V–vi–IV or I–♭VII–IV. Changing genre sets a typical starting tempo (folk 78, blues 92, funk 100, country 104, rock 120, jazz 132, metal 160). You can still drag the slider. MIDI: notes play keys; Start/Stop; CC7 mix; optional pads C1–F1 = Generate / Play / Pause / Stop.

How to use

Backing tracks to improvise over.

Genre locks the chords: blues stays on I, IV and V; jazz leans ii–V–I; funk vamps; metal sits on i–♭VI–♭VII; country is I–IV–V; folk is I–V–vi–IV; rock is I–V–vi–IV or I–♭VII–IV.

Blues has a Major / Minor switch. Major is I7–IV7–V7. Minor is i7–iv7–v7. Still only those three chords.

Explore is a tonic vamp on any Mode Lab scale, so you can hear Hijaz, Pelog and the rest against a backing — it does not dump those scales into blues or metal.

Guitar 6/7/8 or bass 4/5. Same 4th / 2nd / 1st finger boxes as Mode Lab, plus Low/High position and Full / 1st 8ve / 2nd 8ve.
