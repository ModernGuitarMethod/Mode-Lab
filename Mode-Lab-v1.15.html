Ready · pick a key and mode
Haptics pulse on correct notes and beats (supported devices). Rhythm allows about a third of a beat either side of the click and subtracts mic-detect delay, so a correct note is not marked late just because the app heard it a moment later. Background noise is ignored unless it is clearly a different pitch. Change Key and Tuning anytime. Default is guitar, 6-string, shape 2 (2nd finger on the root). Switch to bass for 4- or 5-string in bass clef. Shape is which finger owns the root; Register jumps an octave when it still fits; Root on picks E, A or D.

How to use

This app listens while you play and scores you.

Hit Start listening. Gold is the note to play. Green if you’ve got it. Red if you don’t.

Chromatic tuner is on the same screen.

Pick a scale. Fingering is 4th, 2nd, or 1st finger on the root (one finger per fret, like Monster Scales and Modes). Low/High and which string the root sits on (E/A/D).

Guitar or bass.

Jam this scale sends you to Jam Lab with a backing track.

World includes Japanese, Arabic and Persian names. They are 12-TET guitar approximations (no quarter-tones).

Start listening needs the microphone. Chrome will ask once. If the tuner stays off, allow the mic for this site and try again.
