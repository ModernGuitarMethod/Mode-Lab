How to use it. Jam Lab sits next to Mode Lab. Blues is only I7–IV7–V7 (12-bar when the loop is long enough). Jazz defaults to ii–V–I. Funk vamps on one or two chords. Metal sits on i–♭VI–♭VII power chords. Country is I–IV–V boom-chick. Folk is I–V–vi–IV with quieter drums. Rock is I–V–vi–IV or I–♭VII–IV. MIDI: notes play keys; Start/Stop; CC7 mix; optional pads C1–F1 = Generate / Play / Pause / Stop.
