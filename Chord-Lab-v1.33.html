How to use

  1. Pick the key (home note), then a family, then a mode.
  2. Tap one chord in the diatonic row. That is the chord on the neck.
  3. Read the box (left) and the neck (right). Cyan = root. Gold = chord tone.
  4. Tap a small chord picture under the neck to try another guitar shape of the same chord.
  5. Switch Degrees, Fingers, or Frets to change the labels on the dots.
  6. Tap Play chord to hear the grip. Tap Play 1-3-5 to hear the notes one by one.
  7. About this chord is under the neck. Simple is short. In depth is the full theory.
  8. The five header pills keep this key: Mode · Jam · Chord · Song · Player.
