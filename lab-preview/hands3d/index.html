Rule check

    Rules used

    • One finger per fret in a 4-fret position (index on the position fret).
    • Index or pinky may stretch 1 fret (2 at most, costly).
    • Mode Lab shapes 1-3 for the key are preferred; inside a Mode Lab box the box's own finger numbers are used (B-string adjustment included).
    • Stay in position; shifts cost more with distance and are cheap on rests, long notes, open strings and slides, dear inside hammer/pull runs.
    • Chords: all notes fingered at once, index barres allowed, no crossed fingers.
