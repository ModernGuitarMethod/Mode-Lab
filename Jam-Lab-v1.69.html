How to use it. Jam Lab sits next to Mode Lab. Chords come from the scale you picked (Dorian i is m7, Mixolydian I is 7). Blues is only I–IV–V: major chords in major, minor chords in minor (12-bar when the loop is long enough). Jazz in Ionian is ii–V–I; other modes use that mode’s vamp (Dorian i–IV, Mixolydian I–♭VII). Funk vamps on one or two chords. Metal sits on power chords. Country and folk use the mode’s I–IV–V family. Rock is the mode’s common loop (Ionian I–V–vi–IV, Mixolydian I–♭VII–IV). Changing genre sets a typical starting tempo (reggae 76, folk 78, blues 92, funk 100, country 104, rock 120, jazz 132, metal 160). Generate picks one groove bank so drums, bass and chords match. You can still drag the slider. MIDI: notes play keys; Start/Stop; CC7 mix; optional pads C1–F1 = Generate / Play / Pause / Stop.

How to use

Jam Lab is the backing track next to Mode Lab. Generate picks one groove bank — chords, bass feel, drums, fills and swing stay locked together.

  1. Key / family / mode arrive from Mode Lab (or set them here).
  2. Pick a Genre (Rock default; Reggae, Blues, Jazz, Funk, Metal, Folk, Country, Explore).
  3. Extras in dropdowns: length, chords, voicing, instrument, fingering.
  4. Tap Generate — one bank (progression + bass + drums + fillEvery + feel).
  5. Tap Play (first tap unlocks audio on iOS; SoundFont starts on page open).
  6. Follow the neck: same finger boxes as Mode Lab; cyan root, gold chord tones.
  7. Blues stays I–IV–V; metal diagrams are power 5; reggae skanks offbeats (no rock 2&4 snare).
  8. Use the Mode pill to go back to Test me with the same key.
